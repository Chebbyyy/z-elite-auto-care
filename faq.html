Find answers to the most common questions about our services and processes.

General Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of automotive services including engine diagnostics and repairs, oil and filter changes, brake service, transmission service, tire rotation and balancing, battery replacement, air conditioning service, suspension and steering repairs, full car detailing, interior and exterior detailing, paint correction, ceramic coating, engine steam cleaning, and 24/7 roadside assistance.

We are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including all public holidays. Whether you need an emergency roadside repair at 3am or a scheduled detailing service on Christmas Day, Z Elite Auto Care is here for you.

We are located at 123 Auto Drive, Suite 100, Your City, State 12345. We have a large free customer parking lot on site. We're easy to find, located near the Downtown Mall and City Hospital. Visit our Location page for a map and directions.

Yes! Our certified technicians are trained and experienced to work on virtually all makes and models including domestic and import vehicles, trucks, SUVs, crossovers, and luxury vehicles. We handle everything from economy cars to high-end performance vehicles.
Pricing & Payments

Our oil and filter changes start from $39 for conventional oil. Synthetic oil changes typically start from $59. The exact price depends on your vehicle type, oil specification, and filter required. We'll always give you a precise quote before starting any work.

Yes! All estimates are 100% free and come with no obligation. We believe in complete transparency — you'll always know exactly what your service will cost before we begin any work. We won't start any repairs without your explicit approval.

We accept all major payment methods including cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Zelle. Payment is due at time of service completion. We do not currently offer payment plans, but our pricing is always competitive and transparent.

Absolutely not. We operate on a transparent pricing model. The quote you receive is the price you pay. There are no hidden diagnostic fees, surprise part mark-ups, or mystery labor charges. What we quote is what you pay — period.
Service Process & Timing

A full car detail typically takes between 4 to 8 hours depending on the size of the vehicle, its current condition, and the specific package chosen. Express details can be completed in 2–3 hours. Ceramic coating applications require 1–2 days for full curing time.

Yes! We have a comfortable customer waiting area with free Wi-Fi, coffee, and TV. For longer jobs, we can arrange a courtesy ride to a nearby location or you're welcome to drop off your vehicle and pick it up when it's ready. We'll keep you updated throughout the process.

Our target response time for roadside emergencies within our service area is 30 minutes or less. In most cases, we arrive in 20–25 minutes. Response times may vary slightly during peak hours or adverse weather conditions. Call us at (123) 456-7890 for immediate assistance.
Warranty & Quality

Yes! All mechanical repairs come with a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on both parts and labor. Detailing services come with a satisfaction guarantee — if you're not completely happy with the result, we'll make it right at no charge. Ceramic coatings come with a 2-year warranty.

We use OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) parts or premium aftermarket alternatives that meet or exceed OEM specifications. We never use cheap, low-quality parts. If you have a preference for specific part brands, just let us know when booking and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Yes! Our lead technicians are ASE (Automotive Service Excellence) certified. Our detailing team holds IDA (International Detailing Association) certifications, and our ceramic coating specialists are trained by leading coating manufacturers. We invest continuously in training and technology to stay at the forefront of the industry.
Booking & Appointments

You can book an appointment through our online booking form, by calling us at (123) 456-7890, or by messaging us on WhatsApp. Walk-ins are also welcome — no appointment necessary for most services. We recommend booking in advance for detailing, ceramic coating, and major repairs to secure your preferred time.

Yes! We ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling to avoid any cancellation fees. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a small cancellation fee for large jobs (detailing, ceramic coating). Simply call or text us to reschedule at no charge with adequate notice.
